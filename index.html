# Hello.

I am a web developer and teacher residing in Ottawa, ON. I have a passion for learning, which fuels my desire to teach others.

# Who Am I?

I am a Teacher

For more than a decade, I have been sharing my knowledge with others by teaching web development at Algonquin College. I develop and deliver courses on topics such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, Vue, React, Laravel, C#, and MySQL.

I am a Developer

With over ten years of professional experience, I know how to create websites that function across multiple devices using various technologies.

I am a Father

I became a father in March of 2014 and two more times in June of 2015. I am proud to be a father of three little ones, even if they drive me a little crazy.

I am an Introvert

Yes, I am introverted. I am also focused, self-aware, self-motivated, self-sufficient, detail-oriented, and independent. What I am NOT is an anti-social, nutty hermit.
